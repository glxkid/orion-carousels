=== c09-agent-vs-chatbot

cover ;; Explained ;; Chatbot.|Automation.|*Agent.* ;; What's the difference?
point ;; Chatbot ;; It *answers.* ;; You ask, it replies. It doesn't do anything outside the conversation. ;;  ;; 💬 Talks ;;
